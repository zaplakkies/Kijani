# Kijani Progressive Course

Welcome to the **Kijani Progressive Course**! 

Rather than listing individual hardware features, this course is designed as a **learning journey**. Each lesson introduces one or two new concepts and ends with a project that visibly works. 

Whether you are in a classroom, a scout group, or learning on your own, this course will guide you step-by-step from unboxing to building complex, autonomous robots and systems.

---

## The Learning Journey

The course is organized into seven progressive chapters:

```text
Connect ──> Control ──> Store ──> Make Sound ──> Drive Motors ──> Read Inputs ──> Build Projects
```

1. **[Getting Started](./01-getting-started/intro.md)**: Introduction to Kijani, the web interface, and creating your first web-controlled output.
2. **[Files & Storage](./02-files/uploading.md)**: Uploading and managing code, layout files, and configuration persistently.
3. **[Sound & Music](./03-sound-and-music/sound.md)**: Creating tones, speaker basics, and playing custom RTTTL ringtones.
4. **[Motors](./04-motors/one-motor.md)**: Speed, direction, independent control, and advanced driving algorithms (Tank & Arcade drive).
5. **[Servos](./05-servos/servo-connection.md)**: Controlling continuous and standard servo motors for steering and mechanisms.
6. **[Inputs & Sensors](./06-inputs/digital-inputs.md)**: Digital inputs, bumper switches, collisions, and external analog/digital sensors.
7. **[Projects](./07-projects/build-robot.md)**: Integrating everything to build battlebots, remote-controlled cars, alarms, and custom inventions.
