Kijani at the 2nd Africa Scout Moot: 400 battle bots play soccer in Benoni

July 31, 2026 · 4 min read
Ryan Millard
Kijani Maintainer

From 1–8 August 2026, Rovers from across the continent are descending on Arrowe Park in Benoni, Johannesburg for the 2nd Africa Scout Moot — and Kijani is coming with them. We're bringing 400 boards for two days of robotics on Sunday 2 and Monday 3 August: robot soccer as the main event, with the arena open for pickup battles in the spare time between matches.
